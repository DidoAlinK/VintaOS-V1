========================================================================
  Vinta School OS — Review Build
========================================================================

Thank you for reviewing Vinta School OS before launch.

This is a complete, working copy of the program that runs entirely on
your own computer. Nothing is sent over the internet, and nothing you
do here can affect any other copy of the system.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  HOW TO START
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Double-click:   VintaSchoolOS.exe

  2. A black window opens, and your browser opens the app a moment
     later. If the browser does not open by itself, copy this address
     into your browser:

         http://127.0.0.1:5000

  3. Leave the black window open while you use the app.
     Closing it stops the program.

  If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" warning:
  click "More info", then "Run anyway". This appears because the
  program is not signed with a paid certificate, not because anything
  is wrong with it.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  FIRST TIME: CREATE YOUR ACADEMY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The program starts empty, so that you can set it up the way a real
  academy owner would.

  On the login screen, choose the "Sign up" tab and fill in:
    - your academy name
    - your email address
    - a password (at least 8 characters)
    - a 4-digit PIN

  Your academy is then created, with sensible defaults already set up
  (currency, and four standard payment plans).


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  HOW TO STOP
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Close the black window. That is all — your data is saved
  automatically as you go.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  YOUR DATA
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Everything you enter is stored in the "data" folder, next to the
  program. You can copy that folder to back up your work.

  To start over from empty:
      double-click "Reset Data.cmd"
      (this deletes everything you have entered — be sure)


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  We are looking for anything that feels wrong, confusing, or broken.
  Please try to break it. In particular:

    - Did anything look confusing, or take you too long to work out?
    - Did any screen fail to load, look wrong, or show the wrong words?
    - Did you type something that was accepted but should have been
      rejected — or rejected that should have been accepted?
    - Did any number, date, or amount look wrong?
    - Did you lose work, or did the app close or freeze unexpectedly?
    - Is there anything a real academy owner would need that is
      missing?

  For each problem, it helps a lot if you note:
    - what you were doing when it happened
    - what you expected to happen
    - what actually happened
    - a screenshot, if you can

  Small annoyances are just as useful as crashes. Please report both.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A FEW THINGS THAT ARE EXPECTED (NOT BUGS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - The black window must stay open. If it is closed, the app stops
    and the page will not load.

  - The app only works on this computer. It is not reachable from a
    phone or another PC.

  - "WhatsApp" buttons will not work, because this copy has no
    internet connection to send anything.

  - Arabic text uses your computer's own Arabic font rather than the
    app's font. This is normal in this build.

  - Keep one window open at a time. Opening the program twice just
    brings you back to the copy that is already running.

  - Times and dates shown are in Algeria's time zone.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
  FINALLY
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  This is a pre-release review copy, not the final product. Please
  feel free to be blunt — finding problems now is exactly the point,
  and it is far cheaper than finding them after launch.

  Thank you for your time.
